Vidya Poshak Financial Assistance Application form for SemiProfessional (For B.ScNursing, GNM- Nursing, Physiotherapy, B.Sc- Agriculture, Veterinary Science & Horticulture)-2026-2027

Note: Please read the instructions and eligibility criteria carefully before filling out the form.
Instructions

Please keep the following documents and information ready before filling out the application form:

  1. PUC II Year Marks Card
  2. Student’s Selfie/Photograph
  3. CET Rank Sheet (For B.Sc-Nursing, GNM- Nursing, Physiotherapy, B.ScAgriculture, Veterinary Science & Horticulture)
  4. Agriculture/ Veterinary/ Horticulture (PT Rank)
  5. Latest Electricity Bill
  6. Dwelling Unit Photo (Photo of the student with family members in front of the house)
  7. Ration Card (Both sides)
  8. Income Certificate
  9. Short Video of the House: Please record a short video of your house, starting from the outside and moving inside. The video should cover all rooms, including the kitchen, and must be recorded in landscape mode.
Eligibility Criteria
  • The student should have appeared for the PUC II /12 Grade examination for the first time in 2026
  • Applicants must be aspiring to enroll in a Semi-Professional course such as For B.Sc-Nursing, GNM- Nursing, Physiotherapy, B.Sc- Agriculture, Veterinary Science & Horticulture
  • This application is only for students from Karnataka
  • Should have scored 80% and above in the PUC II examination
  • The total family income should be less than ₹15,000/- per month
  • Parents must be residents of Karnataka
Last date to apply: 30 Jun 2026
